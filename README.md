## ✨ Features

- 🪟 **Glassmorphism design** on a plain black wallpaper, with a **dark and light theme**
- 👥 **4 players** and **5 rounds**, with names you can edit and hide
- 🎯 **Bids from 1 to 8** 
- 🧮 **Automatic score calculation** for every round
- 🙈 **Blind bidding**: players bid one by one, each bid is hidden before the phone is passed on
- 🔒 **Hidden scoreboard** in blind mode, with a small **Show scoreboard** button
- ➕ **Total / Hide total** button, so overall points only appear when you ask
- 🟢🔴 Positive points in **green**, negative points in **red**
- 🔠 **Font size** options: Small, Medium, Large. The whole layout scales with it
- 💾 **Save game** and **History** of your latest 10 games (older ones are deleted automatically)
- ♻️ **Reset game** to start again from round 1
- 📲 Bottom navigation made for phones

## 🎮 How to play

1. Open the **Game** tab and type the 4 player names. Tap **Players** to hide the name boxes.
2. For each round, set every player's **Bid** and **Won** tricks, then tap **Finish round**.
3. Open the **Scoreboard** tab to see each round's points.
4. Tap **Total** to calculate the overall scores, and **Hide total** to hide them again.

### 🙈 Blind bidding

Turn it on in **Settings**.

1. One player's card appears at a time.
2. The player picks a bid and taps **Hide bid and pass to the next player**.
3. After all 4 players have bid, enter only the **tricks won**.
4. Bids are never shown, and the scoreboard stays hidden until you tap **Show scoreboard**.
